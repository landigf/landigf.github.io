What I'd hand you right now: one book, three essays

📚 The book is The Unfair Advantage: How You Already Have What It Takes to Succeed, by Ash Ali and Hasan Kubba. It argues that the self-made story is mostly a myth, and that the useful move is to look honestly at the advantages you already have — money, intelligence, education, location, status, timing — and build on those instead of borrowing someone else's playbook.

✍️ The essays are all by Alexandr Wang, from his newsletter Rational in the Fullness of Time. Short, and they stuck with me:

What I Learned In 2023 — sixteen lessons from a year in AI, on beginner's mindset, focus and deep work.
DO TOO MUCH — on why good teams don't just do the thing, they overdo it.
Optimism Shapes Reality — on how ambitious timelines actually make you faster, not just late.

The Ultimate World Bucket List: How Many Weekends to See the Whole Planet?

🌍 A little side project: I mapped every iconic place and experience on Earth I'd love to see into one interactive dashboard — counted in "weekend-units" so the whole planet becomes a checklist you can actually plan.

The verdict? ~154 destinations, ~199 weekend-units, ~$185k — roughly 4 years if you travelled every single weekend. It's got an interactive world map, live progress you can tick off (saved in your browser), and local know-how for each spot: what to eat, a custom to know, and the faux-pas to avoid.
